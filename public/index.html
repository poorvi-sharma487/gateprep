📝 Study guide

How to prepare each topic

Follow this learning sequence for every topic:

  1. Understand the basic concepts.
  2. Watch a lecture and make short notes.
  3. Solve practice questions without looking at answers.
  4. Attempt relevant GATE previous-year questions.
  5. Revise mistakes and track your progress.
